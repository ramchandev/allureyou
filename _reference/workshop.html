Why pre-pleating

The saree is ready before the bride is.

Pre-pleating means folding and setting a saree's pleats and pallu ahead of time, so on the day it drapes in minutes and stays sharp for hours. It is the quiet skill behind every calm bridal morning, and one of the most requested services in any studio.

Walk in with a saree. Walk out knowing how to make it look like this, without an iron.
Join the 31 Oct batch
What you will learn

Fold, pleat, drape. In that order.

The day follows the same sequence Yashmin uses on a wedding morning. You practise every step with your own hands.

Step one · Folding

Perfect box folding & hanger folding

Set the pleats and pallu so the saree travels and stores without losing its shape.

  • Measuring pleat width to the wearer
  • Box folding for silk and heavy borders
  • Hanger folding for ready-to-wear sarees
Step two · Pleating

Fluffy pleats & fabric handling

Read the fabric and handle it so pleats fall full and even, from soft cotton to stiff Kanchipuram silk.

  • Even, fluffy pleat technique
  • Handling silk, cotton and synthetics
  • Pinning that holds and stays hidden
Step three · Draping

Saree draping without ironing

Take the pre-pleated saree to a finished, camera-ready drape quickly and without an iron.

  • Fast drape from a pre-pleated saree
  • Pallu setting and finishing
  • Keeping the drape sharp all day
